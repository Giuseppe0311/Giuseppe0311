## About Me

I'm a **Backend Engineer focused on financial systems**, with experience building and maintaining software used in banking operations, investments, credit systems and customer platforms.

What interests me most is not technology for technology's sake.

I like understanding the **business problem behind a system**:

- How does money move through the system?
- Where does operational friction exist?
- How can software reduce cost, risk or complexity?
- How does a technical decision affect customers and the business?
- Can an existing financial process be made simpler, faster or more reliable?

I see software engineering as a tool to solve those problems.

My experience has taken me through **mobile banking, treasury operations, financial APIs, credit systems, FIX-based investment flows and analytical CRM platforms**.

Today, I'm especially interested in **fintech, banking infrastructure, payments, financial markets and products that connect technology with real economic activity**.

## 🧠 How I Think About Engineering

```text
Business problem
      ↓
Understand the financial / operational flow
      ↓
Design the simplest reliable solution
      ↓
Build
      ↓
Measure the impact
      ↓
Improve
```

I care about clean architecture and good engineering practices, but I try to avoid overengineering.

A technically impressive system that doesn't solve an important problem is still a bad product.
